Exchange v0.1.0: the first release of the organisation's catalog, holding no entries: the entry format, the manifest, the release contract and the checks a consumer can rely on are what this release pins.

## Entries

- Added: none. Changed: none. Withdrawn: none. The manifest at this release lists no entry; a desk that reads it finds the empty catalog and nothing to import.

## What this release pins

- The entry format of [`docs/entry-format.md`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-exchange/blob/v0.1.0/docs/entry-format.md), mirrored to RFC 0019 §3 as revised in judgment-pack-spec#163: the closed `entry.json` with its six authoring-metadata rows and their bounds, `dialect.json` and `output.json` by direction, the fixtures, and the manifest with its bounds and the release-tag grammar.
- The release contract of [`docs/release-contract.md`](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-exchange/blob/v0.1.0/docs/release-contract.md), RFC 0019 §7 in this repository's words: a release is an annotated tag peeled to its commit, files are read as exact blobs of that commit, and the consumer's checks and refusals are listed there. Where this repository's pages differ from RFC 0019, the RFC is right.
- The checks of `tests/test_entries.py`, run by the `entries` job at this commit with the specification's verifier at the commit CI pins, `f821757fcaf5cb9831dbcb1f8b386a5ead793e4a`: every bound of the metadata rows, the manifest and the binding document, each held by a refused example tree.

## What a consumer must review

- The manifest's `entries` is empty; a consumer that expects entries should treat this release as the format's first pin, not as content.
- The specification's verifier at `f821757fcaf5cb9831dbcb1f8b386a5ead793e4a` reads dialect version 2; an entry whose dialect is of version 3 is held by its form and digest and reported as not recomputable until the pin moves (RFC 0019 §7).

See [releasing](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-exchange/blob/v0.1.0/docs/releasing.md) for how this release is verified by hand: the attestation of `manifest.json` and `checksums.txt`, then `sha256sum --check`, then the comparison with the tagged commit's blob.
