## What this release pins

- Entry format version **3**, as v0.2.0.
- The manifest and exact entry files in the tagged commit, by their byte digests and each Dialect's canonical digest; the immutable release tag is the import boundary.
- The specification verifier at `4bec33ae9ff5947ddcb6b812c558a9aab6ce56d1` (#29), the commit of RFC 0018 round 16 (judgment-pack-spec#215), where v0.2.0 pinned `c51c81de244a0da279f8b926507e3fe2142348d1` and `main` had since moved to `002ab19` (rounds 13 to 15, #28). The reader is unchanged between `4bec33a` and the specification's current `main`. Under the new reader every catalog check answers as before: the per-test results at `002ab19` and at `4bec33a` are identical, and the six refused fixtures that cite the reader were re-pinned with their digests recomputed. The verifier is not used for a preset; the recipe documents are checked by the catalog's own form checker.

## What a consumer must review

- **Round 16's page path form.** A scoped page profile's `endpointScope` entries, and every page URL requested under one, carry no escaped slash or backslash (`%2f`, `%2F`, `%5c`, `%5C`) anywhere, and no segment whose part before its first `;`, `%3b`, `%3B`, `%3f`, `%3F`, `%23` or `%00` is a dot segment; nothing is decoded. A page whose path carries an escaped slash can be read only under an exact-endpoint profile naming that URL. Runner v0.22.0 and later hold both the live and the offline paths to it; the Desk holds its admission and its receipt check to it since judgment-pack-desk#466, and pins Runner v0.23.0 since judgment-pack-desk#485.
- Everything v0.2.0's notes say a consumer must review still holds: these are unexecuted presets with empty `verification` arrays, each recipe pins its model and the `answer`, `search` and `page` capabilities, a portable execution needs installation-owned generated profiles and local connection bindings, and an unpaired preset is refused for running.

All entries are Apache-2.0. Publication follows the signed-tag and protected review process in [releasing](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-exchange/blob/v0.2.1/docs/releasing.md); no release is published by adding these notes.
