Exchange v0.2.0 adds three portable research presets. Each is a starting recipe for local review and binding, with typed requested fields and explicit evidence constraints. None includes a qualification pack, live research results, or an executed fixture.

## Entries

Added:

- `judgmentpack.company-research` — company facts, public evidence, employee ranges and source age, without qualification decisions.
- `judgmentpack.people-at-company` — public professional roles at one explicitly selected company; no inferred contact details or permission to contact anyone.
- `judgmentpack.procurement-opportunity` — public procurement notices, issuers, deadlines and status, without deciding eligibility or bid priority.

Changed: none. Withdrawn: none. The previous release, v0.1.0, had an empty catalog.

## What this release pins

- Entry format version **3**. All three entries have kind `preset`, direction `input`, integer Dialect version **3**, and unbound research sources. Presets have no named-artifact pair: this entry format forbids `artifact` on a preset. A Desk-authored named Dialect is a separately reviewed artifact with its own identity and semantic version.
- The manifest and exact entry files in the tagged commit, including their byte digests and each Dialect's canonical digest. The immutable release tag is the import boundary; branch names and open pull requests are not releases.
- The specification verifier at `c51c81de244a0da279f8b926507e3fe2142348d1`, the commit this release's CI pins. Checks cover entry forms, the three recipe documents, their manifest identities, and catalog history.

## What a consumer must review

These are **unexecuted presets**. Their `verification` arrays and manifest fixture lists are empty. Catalog admission establishes form and identity, not that a model will produce correct data or that a live request has run.

Each recipe pins `gemini-2.5-flash` and the `answer`, `search`, and `page` capabilities. A portable execution needs compatible installation-owned generated profiles and separate local connection bindings. There are no credentials, connection grants, retained website responses, or customer records in the entries. A different model requires an explicitly changed recipe, not silent substitution.

The `request` case parameter supplies the user's research task. Users must choose their scope, sources and limits and review the retained evidence. A people-at-company request must identify the selected company. These presets do not define a portable company-to-person workflow, a reviewer's selection, a job schedule, or outreach execution.

Desk may use an imported preset to start an editable local research draft. That authoring fork is not evidence that the published portable source was executed. Binding a portable source and replaying retained research responses is distinct from starting fresh acquisition; a consumer must expose its actual supported mode.

All entries are Apache-2.0. Publication follows the signed-tag and protected review process in [releasing](../releasing.md); no release is published by adding these notes.
