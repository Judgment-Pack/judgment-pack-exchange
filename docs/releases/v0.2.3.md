Exchange v0.2.3 adds the catalog's first dialect entry, `judgmentpack.directory-identity`. It prepares one directory identity's records, in the shapes the Microsoft Graph API returns, as facts and evidence for an access review pack. It carries synthetic fixtures that two independent verifiers replay. The three presets are unchanged.

## Entries

- **Added:** `judgmentpack.directory-identity` (#56), of kind `dialect`, dialect version 3.
  - It is written against the pack `https://example.com/judgment-packs/directory-identity` at version `0.1.0` (JPS Core 0.5.0), which its README reproduces in full.
  - It prepares **21 facts** under **16 evidence requirements**, one case per identity.
  - Three synthetic fixtures: a member with every stream read, a guest with streams parked, and a service principal.
- **Changed:** none. **Withdrawn:** none.
  - The three entries of v0.2.0, `judgmentpack.company-research`, `judgmentpack.people-at-company` and `judgmentpack.procurement-opportunity`, keep their identities and digests.

## The directory-identity entry

- **What the caller prepares.** The dialect reads one record per identity that the caller groups from receipted reads, as the README's "What the caller prepares" section lists it. It joins nothing and iterates over nothing. The caller prepares the parts a dialect cannot derive:
  - the transitive groups;
  - the privileged role assignments with their definitions;
  - the identity's kind and whether it is deleted;
  - the licence tier;
  - each stream's state.
- **A stream not read is stated, never silent.** Each stream is marked present or absent, and an absence carries one of five reasons: `licence`, `permission`, `read-failed`, `not-applicable` or `home-directory`.
  - An absent stream writes no fact, and its evidence reads absent.
  - A stream read with nothing in it writes `[]`, and its evidence reads present.
  - The two never look alike.
- **Case facts are asserted.** Every fact is copied from the prepared case (RFC 0018 §6.2). The one source read is the organization's assigned plans, through `GET /v1.0/organization`, the path the gateway's `microsoft-graph` binding admits.
- **The pack is illustrative.** Its rules show the facts' use. They are not a recommended access policy.

**The fact names are stable from this release.** These names will not change:
- `principal.*`, `sp.*`, `directory.licence`, `directory.sign_in_event`, `directory.audit_event` and `directory.risk_detection`;
- the absence reason `home-directory`.

Renaming any of them, or changing its meaning, is a breaking change. It is announced in advance.

## What a consumer must review

- **Before running the entry:**
  - the pack's fact meanings against your own;
  - the record your service prepares per identity, against the README;
  - the parked-stream convention.
- **The fixtures are synthetic.** Every id, name and host is invented, and nothing in them was read from a live directory.
- **The presets:** everything v0.2.2's notes say still holds for them.

All entries are Apache-2.0. Publication follows the signed-tag and protected review process in [releasing](https://github.com/Judgment-Pack/judgment-pack-exchange/blob/v0.2.3/docs/releasing.md). Adding these notes publishes no release.
